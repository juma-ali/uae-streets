The grammar of a name

Five ways a street gets its name

UAE street names are not arbitrary. Almost every one belongs to a small number of patterns — learn the patterns and you can read a city quickly.

  1. 01

    People

    Rulers, founders, and members of the ruling families. The biggest roads usually carry the biggest names.

    شارع الشيخ زايدSheikh Zayed Road

  2. 02

    Places

    The district itself, a well, a harbour, a hill, or a landmark the road happens to pass.

    شارع البحيرةBuhairah Corniche

  3. 03

    Words

    Abstract nouns and natural features: connection, unity, renaissance, peace, the Gulf. The street carries a value.

    شارع الوصلAl Wasl Road

  4. 04

    Tribes

    Bani Yas, Al Nuaimi, Al Mualla, Al Sharqi, Al Qasimi — tribal and family names that map the country's social geography onto its roads.

    شارع بني ياسBaniyas Road

  5. 05

    Events

    Dates and national milestones: the federation, National Day, a royal visit. The address becomes a commemoration.

    شارع الثاني من ديسمبر2 December Street

A note on sources

Each entry is labelled. “Documented” means the person, event or landmark is on the record. “Local account” means the explanation is widely told but not officially recorded — we mark it rather than dress it up as fact. Spelling varies too: Al Wasl and Al Wasel are the same word, transliterated twice.

About

From a handful to every road

What began as a handful of roads is now the whole country: all 11,246 named roads have a card with their district, naming pattern, route number and, where a source exists, what the name means. 6,486 carry a meaning, 4,559 of them in both languages. 567 rest on a published source; the rest say plainly what is not known.
